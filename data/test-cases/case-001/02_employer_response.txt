Date: 28 March 2025
From: Mark Thorne (Director, Apex Engineering Ltd)
To: Regulatory Enquiries Team
Reference: Apex Engineering Pension Scheme (Reg: 12904812)

We acknowledge receipt of your letter dated 20 March 2025 regarding alleged missing contributions.

Apex Engineering Ltd experienced a temporary banking software migration failure during November and December 2024 which delayed automatic direct debit transfers to the scheme administrator. 

We dispute Mr Davies' assertion that payments were missing for October 2024. All October contributions were successfully processed on 19 November 2024. The outstanding payments for November 2024 through January 2025 (totalling £38,200 across 18 members) were fully settled on 25 March 2025. HR Manager Sarah Jenkins met with Mr Davies on 12 February 2025 to clarify this situation.
