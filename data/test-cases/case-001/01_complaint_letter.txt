Date: 14 March 2025
From: John Davies (Scheme Member)
To: Regulatory Enquiries Team
Subject: Failure to pay schedule of contributions - Apex Engineering Pension Scheme

I am writing to formally report my employer, Apex Engineering Ltd, for non-payment of pension contributions.
According to my pay slips, pension deductions were taken from my monthly salary of £3,200 between October 2024 and January 2025. However, when I checked my pension portal with the scheme provider on 2 March 2025, no payments had been credited to my account for those four months. 

I raised this directly with our HR Manager, Sarah Jenkins, on 10 February 2025, but received no response. Total missing contributions across all affected staff are estimated to exceed £45,000.
